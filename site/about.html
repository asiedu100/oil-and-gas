About us

A Ghana-registered trading company with a continental ambition

Our story

From a decade of trading to a company of our own

Jabo & Associates Company Ltd is a Ghana-registered company trading in petroleum products, precious and rare earth minerals, fertilizers, solar energy and electric vehicles, with associates around the world.

The company was formally established in October 2021. Its founders had been active in West African commodity trading for more than ten years before that.

Our mission

Fair, stable pricing across Africa’s commodity markets

To build a transparent commodities exchange that brings fair, stable pricing to petroleum products and, through them, to other commodities across Africa.

Our vision

A catalyst for private-sector growth

To be a catalyst for private-sector growth that adds value to Africa’s natural resources, supports governments’ economic and decentralisation goals, and creates income for Africa’s youth.
