About us

Built on more than a decade of West African trade

Founded in 2021 by partners with over a decade of commodity trading experience in West Africa, Jabo & Associates works for fair prices and transparent trade across the continent. Our goal is a private-sector platform that brings stability to Africa’s commodity markets and creates opportunities for its young people.
