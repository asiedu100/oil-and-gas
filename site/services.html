Services

Finance, price transparency and risk management for commodity traders

01

Commodity finance

We help clients manage working capital and liquidity through financing tied to the commodities they trade: commodity-linked loans, pre-export and pre-import financing, and inventory financing.

02

Trade finance

We support cross-border trade with letters of credit, trade finance facilities and precious-mineral collateral arrangements, giving traders and producers liquidity and reduced risk.

03

Price transparency

We give clients clear, up-to-date price information based on market benchmarks, so they can make decisions on supply, demand and market trends, and trade on fair terms.

04

Risk management & hedging

For businesses exposed to price swings, we help structure hedging strategies that lock in prices for future delivery and bring predictability to their operations.

05

Sustainability & ESG

We encourage our partners to follow international ESG standards and support ESG-compliant contracts, green commodities and carbon offset arrangements.
