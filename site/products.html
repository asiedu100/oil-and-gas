Products & commodities

Petroleum, fertilizers and minerals for spot and contract buyers

Through our network of refinery partners, we supply petroleum products for spot and contract purchase. Every cargo is inspected by SGS or an equivalent independent inspector, and prices are negotiated from recognised Platts benchmarks.

How a trade works

Four steps from inquiry to delivery

Inquiry

The buyer or their mandate sends the product, quantity, destination port and delivery terms.

Verification

We confirm the buyer’s identity and financial capability through their bank.

Contract

Both parties agree the contract, including who pays freight, insurance and import or export duties.

Inspection & delivery

The product is inspected by SGS or equivalent before delivery.
